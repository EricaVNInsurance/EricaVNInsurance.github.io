Comparing options

How do I compare my coverage options?

Start with your care and your budget. Then look at how each option fits.

Two different approaches

Original Medicare with Medigap compared with Medicare Advantage
What to compareOriginal Medicare + MedigapMedicare Advantage
How coverage worksOriginal Medicare pays first; Medigap helps with covered cost sharing.A private Medicare-approved plan provides your Medicare benefits.
ProvidersGenerally, providers nationwide who accept Medicare.Network rules vary by plan.
PrescriptionsUsually arranged through a separate Part D plan.Most plans include Part D.
CostsCompare Part B, Medigap, and Part D premiums and remaining costs.Compare premiums, service costs, and the medical out-of-pocket limit.

Make it personal

Check your doctors, prescriptions, travel needs, and budget. Also confirm whether you can enroll in the coverage you are considering.

Neither approach is right for everyone.

Medigap purchase protections and Medicare Advantage enrollment periods follow different rules. Check both before switching.
