Two different approaches

Original Medicare with Medigap compared with Medicare Advantage
What to compareOriginal Medicare + MedigapMedicare Advantage
How coverage worksOriginal Medicare pays first; Medigap helps pay some of your share of covered medical bills.A private Medicare-approved plan provides your Medicare benefits.
ProvidersGenerally, providers nationwide who accept Medicare. Medicare SELECT policies have network restrictions for full supplemental benefits.You may need to use the plan’s doctors and hospitals.
PrescriptionsUsually arranged through a separate Part D plan.Most plans include Part D.
CostsAdd up monthly payments for Part B, Medigap, and Part D, plus your share of care.Check monthly payments, costs for care, and the yearly limit on your share of covered medical bills.
Example: Rosa sees doctors in two states

Rosa spends part of the year in another state and sees doctors in both places. Before choosing coverage, she checks how each option covers routine visits in both locations.

She also compares the medicines she takes and what she may pay. Her travel, doctors, prescriptions, and budget all matter to the comparison.

Make it personal

Check your doctors, prescriptions, travel needs, and budget. Also confirm whether you can enroll in the coverage you are considering.

Neither approach is right for everyone.

The rules about buying Medigap and signing up for Medicare Advantage are different. Check whether you can get the new coverage before changing your current coverage.
