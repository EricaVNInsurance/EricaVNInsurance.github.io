Local coverage

Why does my address matter?

Medicare Advantage plans are offered in specific areas. Your county can change your choices.

Can I get the same plan as a friend?

Maybe. A plan offered in your friend’s county may not be offered in yours, even within the same state. Plans can also change from one year to the next.

What is a service area?

It is the place where a plan is offered. Use your permanent home ZIP code and county to check whether you can join.

What if I move?

A move may change your plan choices and give you a special time to change coverage. The deadline depends on the move and when you tell your plan. Contact the plan before moving when possible.

Two separate questions

Can I join this plan where I live? And how does it cover care when I travel? Check both.
