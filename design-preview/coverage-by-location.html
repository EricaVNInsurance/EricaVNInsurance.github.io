Local coverage

Why does my location matter?

Medicare Advantage plan availability can differ by county—even within the same state.

Plans have service areas

Insurance companies decide where they offer Medicare Advantage plans. A plan available to a friend in another county may not be offered where you live. Plans can also enter or leave an area from one year to the next.

Use your permanent home address

Start a comparison with your ZIP code and county. Confirm your address is in the plan’s service area and check the exact plan, coverage year, and provider network.

Planning a move?

A move can affect available plans and may give you a Special Enrollment Period. The timing and changes allowed depend on the circumstances. Contact your plan before moving when possible.

Before choosing coverage

Confirm plan availability for your address and how you would get care where you expect to use it. Availability at home and coverage while traveling are separate questions.
