## Key distinctions

- State lines are not the same as plan-network boundaries. Verify exact provider and service.
- HMO and HMO-POS: routine network rules versus specified POS benefits; emergency, out-of-area urgently needed care, and temporary dialysis exceptions.
- PPO: out-of-network coverage is not a guarantee that every provider will see members. No specialist referral; in-network prior authorization can apply. CMS FAQ Q11 prohibits required prior authorization for covered PPO out-of-network services; medical necessity and coverage requirements still apply. A voluntary coverage determination is not required authorization.
- Standard Medigap supplements Original Medicare rather than using a Medicare Advantage network; Medicare SELECT restrictions are explicitly identified.
- Routine care, urgent care, follow-up, temporary travel, moving, and foreign care are separated. No fixed plan-specific travel allowance or worldwide routine coverage promise.
- SNP status is an eligibility designation; HMO/PPO format still controls network rules. PFFS, MSA, and Cost Plan differences explained without claiming local availability.
- CMS official comparison plus each plan-type page, CMS coverage FAQ Q11, federal rule on out-of-network access, Medicare.gov foreign-care page, Texas insurance department SELECT explanation, Medigap guide, and Medicare.gov joining-plan rules are linked in the new guide. No carrier, provider, price, or eligibility claims made.
