Enrollment timing

When can I make a change?

The answer depends on what coverage you have, what you want to change, and what has happened.

The annual window

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7. During this period, eligible people can make certain changes to Medicare Advantage or Part D coverage, generally effective January 1.

Special Enrollment Periods

Certain events, such as moving or losing other coverage, may allow changes outside the annual window. Each Special Enrollment Period has its own conditions, deadlines, and permitted changes.

What if I have Medicaid or Extra Help?

Additional enrollment opportunities may apply, but the type of assistance and the change you want to make matter. Do not assume any plan can be changed at any time or that all Medicaid categories have the same rules.

Medigap has separate rules

The fall enrollment window does not guarantee that an insurance company must sell you a Medigap policy. Check Medigap protections separately before changing coverage.

For a timing question

Start with what changed and when. Confirm both the enrollment deadline and the new coverage start date before making a decision.
