Enrollment timing

When can I sign up or change plans?

Medicare has set sign-up times. Certain life changes can give you another chance.

On this page

What is an enrollment period?

It is a window of time when you are allowed to sign up or make certain coverage changes.

What happens each fall?

From October 15 through December 7, eligible people can make certain changes to Medicare Advantage or Part D plans. Those changes generally start January 1.

What if something changes during the year?

Moving or losing other coverage may give you a Special Enrollment Period. That means an extra chance to make certain changes. The deadline and the changes allowed depend on what happened.

What if I have Medicaid or Extra Help?

You may have other chances to change coverage. The rules depend on your assistance and the type of change. It does not mean you can choose any plan at any time.

Medigap follows different rules

The fall sign-up period does not guarantee you can buy a Medigap policy. Check those rules separately. Always confirm both your deadline and when new coverage would start.
