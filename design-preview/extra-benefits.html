Coverage details

What about dental, vision, and hearing?

Extra benefits can be useful. The details determine how useful they are for you.

Look beyond the benefit name

Some Medicare Advantage plans include benefits that Original Medicare generally does not cover, such as routine dental, vision, or hearing services. Availability and coverage vary by plan.

Questions worth asking

  • What specific services are included?
  • Which providers can I use?
  • Are there copays or coverage limits?
  • Do any eligibility requirements apply?
  • Where is the benefit explained in the plan documents?

Keep the rest of your care in view

Compare these benefits alongside your doctors, medications, and medical costs. A benefit that sounds appealing may be less useful if the plan does not work well for the care you already receive.

Check the exact plan and year.

A benefit advertised for one plan or location does not establish what another plan covers. Review the Summary of Benefits and Evidence of Coverage before deciding.
