Coverage details

What about dental, vision, and hearing?

Some Medicare Advantage plans include extra benefits. The details vary.

What should I ask?

Check exactly what the plan pays for. A benefit name alone does not tell you how much help you will get.

  • Which services are included?
  • Which dentists, eye doctors, or other providers can I use?
  • What will I have to pay?
  • Is there a limit on what the plan pays?
  • Do I have to meet extra requirements?
An example of what to check

If you need dental work, ask whether that exact treatment is covered, which dentist you can use, and how much of the bill would be yours.

What else matters?

Check your regular doctors, medicines, and medical costs too. A benefit offered by one plan or in one county may not be available with another. Use the plan’s current benefit summary and coverage documents.
