Help with costs

What is Extra Help?

A program that helps eligible people pay Medicare Part D prescription drug costs.

What it helps pay

Extra Help can reduce Part D premiums, deductibles, and prescription cost sharing for people with limited income and resources. You do not pay a Part D late enrollment penalty while receiving Extra Help.

How people qualify

Some people receive it automatically, including people with full-benefit Medicaid, certain Medicare Savings Program assistance, or Supplemental Security Income. Others can apply through Social Security.

Income and resource rules can change. Check the current requirements rather than assuming you do not qualify.

Is it the same as Medicaid?

No. Extra Help is assistance with Medicare drug costs. Receiving it does not by itself mean you have full Medicaid coverage or qualify for every D-SNP.

Keep your notices.

Medicare and Social Security letters can help explain your assistance. If pharmacy costs look wrong, contact your drug plan and ask what proof it needs.
