Help with costs

What is Extra Help?

A Medicare program that lowers prescription drug costs for people who qualify.

What does it help pay?

It can lower your Part D monthly payments, deductible, and what you pay for covered prescriptions. You do not pay a Part D late sign-up penalty while you receive Extra Help.

How do I get it?

Some people get it automatically through full Medicaid, certain help with Medicare costs, or Supplemental Security Income. Others can apply through Social Security. Income and resource limits can change.

Does it mean I have Medicaid?

No. Extra Help by itself does not mean you have full Medicaid or can join every plan for people with Medicare and Medicaid.

If your pharmacy price looks wrong

Contact your drug plan. Ask whether it has your current Extra Help information and what proof it needs.
