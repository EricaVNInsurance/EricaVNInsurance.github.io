Medicaid by state

Find your state’s Medicaid program.

Different names. A clear place to start.

Medicaid has different names across the country. Use this list to recognize the name on a card or letter and find official state information.

Some names cover the whole program; others refer to a particular coverage group or managed-care system. This is a guide to common names, not a list of every plan, waiver, or eligibility category. A program name alone does not tell you which services you qualify for.

CHIP is separate children’s coverage, although some states share a brand with Medicaid. An application portal, state agency, and insurance company can each have a different name.

50 states and Washington, D.C.

For application status, renewals, replacement cards, or eligibility decisions, contact your state agency. You can also use the federal state-agency directory for contact and application options.
