What is Medicare Advantage?

A private Medicare-approved plan provides your Medicare health benefits.

People and situations in the examples are fictional. Dollar amounts are made up to explain the ideas, not a plan quote.

How does it work?

Medicare Advantage is also called Part C. It provides your Part A and Part B benefits. Most plans include prescription drug coverage. You must have Part A and Part B, live where the plan is offered, and sign up during an allowed time.

What should I check?

Check your doctors, medicines, and what you would pay for care. Many plans use a network: a group of doctors and hospitals that work with the plan. Some care may need the plan’s approval first.

Example: Luis wants to keep both doctors

Luis wants to keep his primary doctor and the specialist he sees regularly. A plan includes his primary doctor in its network, but not his specialist.

Checking only one doctor would miss that difference. Luis checks each doctor, his hospital, and the plan’s rules for visits outside its network.

Does a $0 monthly plan payment mean free care?

No. You generally still owe the Part B monthly payment unless assistance or a plan benefit helps pay it. You may also pay when you get care.

A limit worth checking

Plans set a yearly limit on your share of covered Part A and Part B services. Prescription costs follow separate rules. Dental, vision, and other extra benefits vary by plan.
