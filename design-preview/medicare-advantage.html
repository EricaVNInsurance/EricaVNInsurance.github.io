What is Medicare Advantage?

Another way to receive your Medicare benefits, with plan details worth checking.

What it is

Medicare Advantage, also called Part C, is coverage through a Medicare-approved private insurance company. You must have Part A and Part B, live in the plan’s service area, and have an available enrollment period to join. Most plans include Part D drug coverage.

What to check

  • Doctors and hospitals: Confirm the plan’s network and rules for care outside it.
  • Medications: Check the covered drug list, pharmacy, and coverage rules.
  • Costs: Look at copays, coinsurance, and the yearly limit for covered Part A and Part B services.
  • Care requirements: Some services may need a referral or approval from the plan.

What about extra benefits?

Some plans offer dental, vision, or hearing benefits. The details and limits vary. Those benefits are one part of the decision, alongside access to care and overall costs.

A $0 plan premium does not mean all care is free.

You generally still pay the Part B premium unless you receive assistance or a benefit that helps pay it. Other costs may apply.
