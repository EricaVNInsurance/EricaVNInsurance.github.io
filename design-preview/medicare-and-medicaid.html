Can I have Medicare and Medicaid?

Yes, if you qualify for both. The kind of Medicaid help you have matters.

On this page

What is the difference?

Medicare is federal health insurance. Medicaid is run by states under federal rules. It helps eligible people with limited income and resources. When you have both, Medicare generally pays first for Medicare-covered care.

Does everyone get the same help?

No. Some people have full Medicaid coverage. Others have help with certain Medicare costs through a Medicare Savings Program. Extra Help is a separate program for prescription costs. Having Extra Help alone does not mean you have full Medicaid.

What is a D-SNP?

It is a type of Medicare Advantage plan for people who meet that plan’s Medicare and Medicaid requirements. Each plan accepts certain Medicaid categories, or types of assistance.

What to check first

Confirm your current type of assistance, which plans accept it, and when you are allowed to sign up. A Medicaid card alone does not answer all three questions.
