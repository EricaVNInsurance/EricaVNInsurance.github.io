Help with costs

What if I have Medicare and Medicaid?

Start with the type of assistance you have. It affects what help and coverage may be available.

The programs do different things

Medicare is federal health insurance. Medicaid is a joint federal and state program with eligibility and benefits that vary by state. When you have both and receive a Medicare-covered service, Medicare generally pays first.

Not every assistance program is the same

Full Medicaid coverage and Medicare Savings Programs provide different kinds of help. Some programs help pay the Part B premium; others also help with Medicare cost sharing. Extra Help focuses on Part D prescription costs. Having Extra Help by itself does not establish that you have full Medicaid.

What is a D-SNP?

A Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan is a type of Medicare Advantage plan for people who meet its Medicare and Medicaid eligibility requirements. Which Medicaid categories a plan accepts must be checked for that plan.

Before comparing plans

Confirm your current assistance category, the plan’s eligibility rules, and the enrollment period that applies. A Medicaid card alone does not answer every coverage or timing question.
