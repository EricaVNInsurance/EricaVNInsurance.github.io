Costs

What can Medicare cost?

Look at the full picture: what you pay each month and what you pay when you use care.

Four terms you will see

Premium
A regular payment to keep coverage, usually monthly.
Deductible
An amount you pay before coverage begins paying for certain services or drugs.
Copayment
A set dollar amount for a covered service or prescription.
Coinsurance
A percentage of a covered cost that you pay.

Compare more than the premium

A plan with a lower monthly payment can still have costs when you visit a specialist, receive hospital care, or fill prescriptions. Think about routine care and what you could afford in a year when you need more care.

Understand the limits

Original Medicare on its own has no annual out-of-pocket maximum. Medicare Advantage has a yearly limit for covered Part A and Part B services; Part D drug costs follow separate rules. Premiums and services a plan does not cover generally do not count toward its medical limit.

Use the correct coverage year.

Premiums, deductibles, and plan details can change each year. Check the current Medicare figures and the specific plan documents rather than relying on an old price.
