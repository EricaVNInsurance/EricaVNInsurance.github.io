What happens after you reach out?

We will start with your question, then work through the details that matter to you.

01

You tell me what's going on

Start with the situation in your own words. You don't need to know the insurance terminology.

02

We figure out what matters

Depending on your question, that may include doctors, prescriptions, costs, coverage, or timing.

03

We look at your next step

That might mean comparing options, checking eligibility or timing, or simply answering the question you came in with.

Before we talk

A little preparation can help.

Keep your current coverage information, doctor names, medication list, and any recent insurance notices nearby for your conversation. You do not need to email those details to get started.

Keep your first email simple.

Share your first name and a general question. Do not send your Medicare number, Social Security number, financial details, or medical records by email.
