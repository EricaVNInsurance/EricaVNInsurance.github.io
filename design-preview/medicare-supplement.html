What is Medigap?

Extra insurance that helps pay some bills left to you by Original Medicare.

On this page
How does it work?Is it the same as Medicare Advantage?When can I buy it?

How does it work?

Original Medicare pays its share first. Your Medigap policy then helps pay certain costs that are your responsibility. What it pays depends on the policy you buy. You generally need Part A and Part B.

Is it the same as Medicare Advantage?

No. Medigap works with Original Medicare. It does not pay your share of Medicare Advantage bills. New Medigap policies do not include prescription drug coverage.

When can I buy it?

Your federal six-month Medigap sign-up window starts the first month you are 65 or older and have Part B. During that window, health problems cannot be used to deny you a policy the company sells or charge you more.

Check before switching

Outside that window, an insurer may review your health and deny an application or charge more. Certain situations and state laws give extra protections. Rules for people under 65 also vary by state.
