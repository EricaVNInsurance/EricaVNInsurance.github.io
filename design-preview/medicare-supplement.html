What is Medicare Supplement Insurance?

Medigap helps pay certain costs left to you under Original Medicare.

How it fits

Medicare Supplement Insurance, commonly called Medigap, works alongside Original Medicare. Depending on the policy, it helps pay costs such as deductibles, copayments, or coinsurance. You generally need Part A and Part B to buy it.

It is different from Medicare Advantage

Medigap supplements Original Medicare. It does not pay your share of Medicare Advantage costs. New Medigap policies do not include prescription drug coverage; a separate Part D plan may be needed.

Timing matters

Your federal six-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period starts the first month you are 65 or older and have Part B. During that window, insurers cannot deny you a policy they sell because of health problems or charge more because of those problems.

Outside that window, medical underwriting may apply unless you have a guaranteed issue right or additional state protections. Rules for people under 65 differ by state.

Check before changing coverage.

Being able to leave Medicare Advantage does not automatically mean you can buy any Medigap policy you want.
