Common questions in Michigan.

Can I get help without visiting an office?

Erica offers assistance by phone. You can request a call to discuss your questions about coverage or enrollment. The request form is currently a demonstration and does not send requests yet.

What if I don’t know what coverage or assistance I have?

You can start with that question. Erica can help identify what needs to be checked, while the state program can confirm your Medicaid or assistance status. Keep account and member numbers out of the chat.

Does this page list every plan available to me?

No. This is a starter resource guide, not a complete plan directory or an eligibility determination. For all Medicare plan options, use Medicare.gov. Your personalized PlanEnroll link provides a separate self-service route for the options offered through that site.
