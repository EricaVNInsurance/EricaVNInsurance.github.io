Medicare Basics

What is Original Medicare?

Part A and Part B, provided through the federal Medicare program.

What does it include?

Part A helps pay for hospital care. Part B helps pay for doctor visits and other medical care. You can generally use any doctor or hospital in the U.S. that accepts Medicare.

Will I still have bills?

Yes. Medicare pays its part of a covered bill, and you pay your part. Original Medicare alone has no yearly limit on what you could pay for covered care.

What can I add?

You can join a separate Part D plan for prescription medicines. You may also buy Medicare Supplement Insurance, called Medigap, to help pay some of your share of medical bills. Rules about when you can buy Medigap apply.

The main idea

Original Medicare is your hospital and medical coverage. Drug coverage and Medigap are separate choices.
