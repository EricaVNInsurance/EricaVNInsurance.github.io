Medicare Basics

What is Original Medicare?

Original Medicare is the traditional way to receive Medicare coverage directly through the federal Medicare program.

What does it include?

Original Medicare includes Part A and Part B.

Part A

Helps cover hospital and certain related care.

Part B

Helps cover doctor services, outpatient care, preventive services, and more.

What about prescription drugs?

Original Medicare does not automatically include Part D prescription drug coverage. If you want Medicare drug coverage, you can join a separate Part D plan.

What about out-of-pocket costs?

With Original Medicare, Medicare pays its share of covered services and you pay your share. Original Medicare alone has no annual out-of-pocket maximum. Some people choose additional coverage, such as Medicare Supplement Insurance (Medigap), to help with certain out-of-pocket costs.

Can I use any doctor?

With Original Medicare, you can generally see any doctor or hospital in the United States that accepts Medicare.

How is this different from Medicare Advantage?

Medicare Advantage is an alternative way to receive your Medicare benefits through a Medicare-approved private plan. Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage are two different ways to get Medicare coverage.

The main idea:

Original Medicare = Part A + Part B. You can add Part D drug coverage separately, and you may choose additional supplemental coverage.
