Medicare Basics

What are Parts A, B, C & D?

The letters can make Medicare sound more complicated than it is. Each one is just a label for a different part of coverage.

The quick version

Part A

Hospital insurance.

Part B

Medical insurance.

Part C

Medicare Advantage.

Part D

Prescription drug coverage.

Part A: Hospital Insurance

Part A helps cover inpatient hospital care. It can also help cover certain skilled nursing facility care, hospice care, and some home health care.

Part B: Medical Insurance

Part B helps cover services from doctors and other health care providers, outpatient care, certain medical equipment, home health care, and many preventive services.

Part C: Medicare Advantage

Part C is another name for Medicare Advantage. These are Medicare-approved plans from private insurance companies. They provide your Part A and Part B benefits, and most plans also include Part D prescription drug coverage.

Part D: Prescription Drug Coverage

Part D helps cover prescription drug costs. If you use Original Medicare, you can add a separate Part D plan. Many Medicare Advantage plans include Part D already.

One easy way to remember it:

A = hospital, B = medical, C = Medicare Advantage, D = drugs.
