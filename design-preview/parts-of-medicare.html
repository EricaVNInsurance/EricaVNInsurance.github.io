Medicare Basics

What do A, B, C, and D mean?

Each letter tells you something different about your Medicare coverage.

On this page
Part APart BPart CPart D

Part A

Hospital care

Helps pay for care when you are formally admitted to a hospital as an inpatient. It also helps cover certain skilled nursing care, hospice, and home health care.

Part B

Doctor visits and other care

Helps pay for doctor visits, tests, care without a hospital admission, certain medical equipment, and many services that help prevent illness.

Part C

Medicare Advantage

A private Medicare-approved plan provides your Part A and Part B benefits. Most plans also include Part D.

Part D

Prescription medicines

Helps pay for covered prescription medicines. You can get it through a separate drug plan or a Medicare Advantage plan that includes it.

How the pieces fit

Original Medicare is Part A + Part B. Medicare Advantage, or Part C, is another way to get those benefits. Part C is not an extra policy you add to Original Medicare.
