Comparing options

Why can two plans be so different?

Compare the exact plan, rather than relying on the insurance company’s name.

Start with the plan type

HMO and PPO plans can have different rules for networks, referrals, and care outside the network. Special Needs Plans have additional eligibility requirements. A familiar company name does not tell you which rules apply.

Then compare the details

  • Care: Are your doctors and hospitals available under the plan’s rules?
  • Medications: Are your prescriptions covered, and are approvals or other restrictions required?
  • Cost: What will you pay for the services and prescriptions you use?
  • Location: Does the plan serve your permanent home address?

Make sure the comparison matches

Use the same coverage year, address, medication list, and providers for each option. Keep the exact plan name and plan ID with your notes so similarly named plans do not get mixed up.

One detail can change the decision.

If a specialist or prescription matters to you, check it directly rather than assuming another plan from the same company handles it the same way.
