Comparing options

Why are Medicare plans different?

Two plans from the same company can have different doctors, costs, and rules.

People and situations in the examples are fictional. Dollar amounts are made up to explain the ideas, not a plan quote.

What can change from plan to plan?

Check the doctors and hospitals you can use, the medicines covered, and what you pay. Also check whether you need a doctor’s referral or the plan’s approval for certain care.

What do HMO and PPO mean?

These are types of Medicare Advantage plans. HMOs usually require you to use the plan’s doctors and hospitals, with exceptions such as emergency care. PPOs generally allow covered care outside the plan’s network, but it may cost more. Check the exact plan’s rules.

Example: Elena compares similar plan names

Elena sees two plans from the same company with similar names. Her specialist is in only one plan’s network.

She checks the full plan name and details instead of assuming that both plans cover visits to the same doctors.

How do I compare fairly?

Use the same home address, year, doctors, and medicine list for each plan. Keep the full plan name and ID number with your notes.
