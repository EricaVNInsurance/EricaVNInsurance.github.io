Common questions

Before you reach out.

Do I need to choose a plan first?

No. You can ask a question, request help comparing options, or ask about coverage you already have.

What if I don’t know what coverage I have?

That’s okay. Choose “Another Medicare question / I’m not sure.” You do not need all the answers before asking for help.

Do I need my documents ready?

No. Insurance information, doctor names, and a medicine list can help later, but you do not need to gather them before requesting a call.

Does this change my plan or book an appointment?

No. A request does not enroll you in a plan or change your coverage. A preferred call time is not a booking. An appointment is confirmed separately through a scheduling service.

Will I receive texts or have my details sent to other agents?

This form does not ask for permission for text messages or sharing your inquiry with other agents. Optional email permission is separate from your request for a phone call.
