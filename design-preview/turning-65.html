Turning 65

Turning 65? Start here.

Check when to sign up, then decide how you want to get your Medicare coverage.

When can I sign up?

For most people turning 65, the first sign-up window lasts seven months: three months before your birthday month, your birthday month, and three months after. The date your coverage starts depends on when you sign up.

What if I am still working?

Check with your job’s benefits office before delaying Medicare. Insurance from a current job, retiree insurance, and COBRA follow different rules. If you put money into a health savings account, ask how Medicare would affect those payments.

Do I get Medicare automatically?

Some people do. Others must sign up. Check your Social Security or Medicare notices to see what applies to you.

Your first task

Confirm when your Part A and Part B will start. Then compare coverage using your doctors, medicines, and budget.
