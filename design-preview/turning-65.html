Turning 65

New to Medicare? Start here.

You do not need to choose a plan before you understand the steps.

Start with your enrollment timing

For most people approaching 65, the first enrollment window lasts seven months: the three months before your birthday month, your birthday month, and the three months after. Your coverage start date depends on when you enroll.

Still working?

If you or your spouse have coverage through current employment, check how it works with Medicare before delaying Part B. Employer coverage, retiree coverage, and COBRA do not all follow the same rules. Ask your benefits administrator about coordination and any health savings account contributions.

Then look at your coverage choices

Once your Part A and Part B timing is clear, learn how Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage work. Consider doctors, medications, budget, and any other coverage you want to keep.

A useful first step

Find your current insurance information and any Medicare or Social Security notices. Check whether you are already being enrolled; do not assume it happens automatically for everyone.
