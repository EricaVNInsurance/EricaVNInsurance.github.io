Medicare Basics

What is Medicare?

Health insurance mainly for people age 65 and older. Some people qualify at a younger age.

On this page

What does it help pay for?

Medicare helps pay for health care, such as hospital stays and doctor visits. You may still have bills to pay. What you pay depends on your coverage and the care you receive.

What are my main choices?

You can get your Medicare health benefits through Original Medicare or a Medicare Advantage plan. These are two different ways to get Medicare coverage.

Where do the letters fit?

Parts A, B, C, and D describe different parts of Medicare. See what each letter means.
