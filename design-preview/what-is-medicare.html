Medicare Basics

What is Medicare?

Medicare is federal health insurance. Most people become eligible around age 65, although some people can qualify earlier because of certain disabilities or health conditions.

The simple version

You can think of Medicare as a health insurance system made up of different parts. Each part helps with a different type of coverage.

You do not need to learn everything at once.

Start with the basic pieces, then look at how you want to receive your coverage.

What does Medicare help cover?

Medicare can help cover things like hospital care, doctor visits, outpatient care, and prescription drugs. Which part pays depends on the service and the type of coverage you have.

Who is Medicare for?

Medicare is mainly for people age 65 and older. Some people under 65 can also qualify because of certain disabilities or specific health conditions.

How do people get Medicare coverage?

There are two main ways to receive Medicare health coverage:

Original Medicare

Part A and Part B provided through the federal Medicare program.

Medicare Advantage

A Medicare-approved private plan that provides your Part A and Part B benefits, and usually includes Part D drug coverage.

Where do Parts A, B, C & D fit in?

Those letters are just names for different parts of Medicare. You can learn them one at a time.
